# 📚 Referensi Ilmiah — Teknik Grounding

## Referensi untuk PRD & Landasan Teori

### Teknik Grounding

1. Najavits, L. M. (2002). _Seeking Safety: A Treatment Manual for PTSD and Substance Abuse_. Guilford Press.
   - Sumber utama untuk teknik grounding dalam konteks trauma dan PTSD
   - Mengkategorikan grounding menjadi: mental, fisik, dan soothing

2. Linehan, M. M. (2014). _DBT Skills Training Manual_ (2nd ed.). Guilford Press.
   - Distress tolerance skills termasuk teknik grounding sensorik
   - Konsep "wise mind" dan mindfulness

3. Ogden, P., Minton, K., & Pain, C. (2006). _Trauma and the Body: A Sensorimotor Approach to Psychotherapy_. W.W. Norton.
   - Pendekatan somatik untuk grounding
   - Body-oriented interventions

### Teori Pendukung

#### Polyvagal Theory

4. Porges, S. W. (2011). _The Polyvagal Theory: Neurophysiological Foundations of Emotions, Attachment, Communication, and Self-regulation_. W.W. Norton.
   - Bagaimana sistem saraf vagus memediasi respons fight-flight-freeze
   - Grounding sebagai cara mengaktifkan ventral vagal (safety system)

#### Window of Tolerance

5. Siegel, D. J. (2012). _The Developing Mind: How Relationships and the Brain Interact to Shape Who We Are_ (2nd ed.). Guilford Press.
   - Konsep "window of tolerance" — zona optimal regulasi emosi
   - Grounding membantu mengembalikan individu ke window of tolerance

#### Mindfulness-Based Approaches

6. Kabat-Zinn, J. (2013). _Full Catastrophe Living: Using the Wisdom of Your Body and Mind to Face Stress, Pain, and Illness_ (Revised ed.). Bantam Books.
   - MBSR dan elemen mindfulness dalam grounding
   - Body scan meditation sebagai teknik grounding

7. Segal, Z. V., Williams, J. M. G., & Teasdale, J. D. (2013). _Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression_ (2nd ed.). Guilford Press.
   - Integrasi mindfulness dengan pendekatan kognitif

### Teknik Spesifik

#### Breathing Exercises

8. Ma, X., Yue, Z. Q., Gong, Z. Q., Zhang, H., Duan, N. Y., Shi, Y. T., ... & Li, Y. F. (2017). The effect of diaphragmatic breathing on attention, negative affect and stress in healthy adults. _Frontiers in Psychology_, 8, 874.
   - Evidence untuk efektivitas teknik breathing pada stres dan affect negatif

9. Zaccaro, A., Piarulli, A., Laurino, M., Garbella, E., Menicucci, D., Neri, B., & Gemignani, A. (2018). How breath-control can change your life: A systematic review on psycho-physiological correlates of slow breathing. _Frontiers in Human Neuroscience_, 12, 353.
   - Review sistematis tentang efek slow breathing pada psikofisiologi

#### Progressive Muscle Relaxation

10. Jacobson, E. (1938). _Progressive Relaxation_. University of Chicago Press.
    - Sumber asli untuk PMR

11. Manzoni, G. M., Pagnini, F., Castelnuovo, G., & Molinari, E. (2008). Relaxation training for anxiety: a ten-years systematic review with meta-analysis. _BMC Psychiatry_, 8(1), 41.
    - Meta-analisis efektivitas teknik relaksasi untuk kecemasan

### Kesehatan Mental Mahasiswa

12. Auerbach, R. P., Mortier, P., Bruffaerts, R., Alonso, J., Benjet, C., Cuijpers, P., ... & Kessler, R. C. (2018). WHO World Mental Health Surveys International College Student Project: Prevalence and distribution of mental disorders. _Journal of Abnormal Psychology_, 127(7), 623-638.
    - Data prevalensi gangguan mental pada mahasiswa secara global

### Digital Mental Health / mHealth

13. Bakker, D., Kazantzis, N., Rickwood, D., & Rickard, N. (2016). Mental health smartphone apps: review and evidence-based recommendations for future developments. _JMIR Mental Health_, 3(1), e7.
    - Rekomendasi berbasis bukti untuk pengembangan app kesehatan mental

14. Linardon, J. (2020). Can acceptance, mindfulness, and self-compassion be learned by smartphone apps? A systematic and meta-analytic review of randomized controlled trials. _Behavior Therapy_, 51(4), 646-658.
    - Meta-analisis efektivitas mindfulness/acceptance melalui app

### Trauma-Informed Design

15. Chen, A. T., Carreiro, S., Guo, B., Olsen, H., & Boyer, E. W. (2022). Designing trauma-informed digital mental health applications. _AMIA Annual Symposium Proceedings_, 2022, 312-321.
    - Prinsip desain trauma-informed untuk aplikasi digital
